## 4. Bot traps
- `::startroom::Horne` is invulnerable from the lobby on: `c_setinvulnerable(true)` @23036626. She is story; killing her is refused.
- The rooftop suits `::teleport::max`, `p1`..`p4` and `invisible01` are invulnerable by script: @23038813, @23039020, @23039356, @23039626, @23039896, @23040166, and `::teleport::max` toggles `C_SetImmortal(true)` @22917937 / `(false)` @22920816 around the cinematics. `Activator->C_SetInvulnerable(false)` @22965770 is the one that clears it.
- `::Outside::horn1::piece1` and `horn2::piece1` are set `make_unbreakable` at cinematic 2 (@22876892/@22876948). exits.json's `!::Outside::horn1::piece1->FSM_Send(break);` and `!::Outside::horn2::piece1->FSM_Send(break);` are sent in that state, so they do nothing; the real breaker is `::Outside::explosion_detector1->FSM_Send(make_breakable)` then `(break)` @22911897 and @22912939, which is exactly what the same `ff` block sends afterwards.
- Door traps: every `door_dummy1::door_trigger` (startroom @22815312, mediroom @22815312-22815586, middle @22822727) plays `door1.DO->do_animate(delay)` and then re-runs `check_function`; if the room is not cleared the door shuts again. A bot that fires the trigger remotely gets an open door it never walks through, then a closed one.
- `::ramp::ct1` @22978749 teleports Horne to `::Outside::w1`, not to w5: the `c_teleport("::Outside::w5")` in exits.json duplicates work the script already does at `::Outside::ct3` @22892449, and doing it early moves Horne before `combat_script` starts @22979309.
- `::Outside::KATTOSYS::open_door_trigger` @22941195 starts `cin1_cam1`, which turns controls off @22883272 and the player invulnerable @22883376. Sending it while the player is elsewhere plays the whole rooftop cinematic with the player frozen in the hut.
- `::Outside::explosion_detector1` is a `.DO` with hitpoints 1 (@22910731) that alternates breakable/unbreakable by FSM; shooting it during `unbreakable` (@22912250) does nothing, so a bot that waits on its death must wait for the `breakable` state.
