## Findings (web, Chrome headless, 2026-11-05)
- Every weapon equips by its slot key, shows its name and ammo in the HUD, and consumes rounds (beretta 162->144 after 6 shots, grenade/molotov 10->9).
- Bullet time: LShift tap (hold 150 ms, no move axis) switches it on, the meter drains 1.00 -> 0.00 in about 800 frames (15 s SlowMotionTime), then it ends by itself.
- Pitch: during bullet time the 3D sounds are set to 0.57-0.58x through SetFrequency (200 of 200 logged changes), 2D/music stay at 1.0x. The expected 0.32x at scale 0.2
  (bullettime.md 3) is not measured here: the scale reached during the run was not read, so the exact value is NOT VERIFIED; the detune itself works.
- Not exercised: sniper scope zoom (key Q) and the bullet cam on a kill; the OWNER should judge those with the sniper link and Q.
- A pickup alone does not equip (weapon_reads.md): the `@k:<slot>` line is part of every link. `w` in the ghost line is the real id once equipped.
- No weapon failed, so no game patch; fixes in this commit are tooling: web env split, SetFrequency log (RN_SOUNDLOG).
